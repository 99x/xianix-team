# Intents

> **From Traditional Agile:** Epics (Large, static backlogs of pre-planned work)  
> **To AI-DLC:** Intents (High-level statements of business purpose that AI decomposes into actionable tasks)

In traditional Agile, the *Epic* reigns supreme. Teams spend countless hours meticulously breaking down massive, static backlogs of pre-planned work into digestible user stories. It's a heavy, human-driven process that attempts to predict the future and prescribe every task.

AI-DLC fundamentally shifts this paradigm with **Intents**. Instead of micromanaging the "how," you focus entirely on the "why" and the "what." An Intent is a high-level, declarative statement of business purpose. 

Rather than humans passively assisting the process, an AI agent takes the wheel. It ingests the Intent and autonomously decomposes it into context-aware, actionable tasks in real-time. You declare the destination, and the AI charts the optimal, dynamic path to get there, eliminating backlog fatigue and accelerating delivery.
