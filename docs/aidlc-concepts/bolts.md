# Bolts

> **From Traditional Agile:** Sprints (Multi-week planned delivery cycles)  
> **To AI-DLC:** Bolts (Rapid, intense cycles of continuous build-validation lasting hours or days)

The traditional Agile *Sprint* was designed for human cadence. It locks scope into rigid, multi-week delivery cycles, creating artificial boundaries where delivery happens at the end of a slow, predictable runway.

Enter the **Bolt**. In the AI-DLC era, development velocity isn't constrained by two-week ceremonies. A Bolt is a rapid, intense cycle of continuous build, test, and validation that collapses the timeline from weeks down to hours or, at most, a few days. 

Because AI agents can write boilerplate, execute test suites, and refactor at machine speed, Bolts focus on relentless, immediate impact. It's an explosive burst of productivity where software is continuously validated, shifting the mindset from "what can we deliver in two weeks" to "what can the AI ship right now."
