# Mob Elaborations

> **From Traditional Agile:** Standups (Status update meetings for human coordination)  
> **To AI-DLC:** Mob Elaboration (Real-time rituals where the team validates AI-generated proposals live on a shared screen)

The traditional Agile *Standup* is often a passive reporting ritual. It's a status update meeting built for human coordination, frequently devolving into repetitive "what I did yesterday, what I'll do today" checklists.

In AI-DLC, the Standup evolves into **Mob Elaboration**. This is an active, real-time ritual where the team gathers—not to report status—but to validate AI-generated proposals live on a shared screen.

The AI has already done the heavy lifting: analyzing intents, prototyping solutions, and proposing architectural shifts. During a Mob Elaboration, the human team acts as editors and directors. You instantly review, steer, and approve the AI's momentum collaboratively. It's high-bandwidth, highly engaging, and shifts the team from passive reporters to active decision-makers.
