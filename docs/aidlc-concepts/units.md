# Units

> **From Traditional Agile:** Subdomains (Conceptual groupings within a bounded context)  
> **To AI-DLC:** Units (Cohesive, self-contained elements designed for independent deployment and measurable value)

Domain-Driven Design and traditional Agile often organize complex systems into *Subdomains*—conceptual groupings within a bounded context. While conceptually useful for human understanding, in practice, these often grow into entangled monoliths that are difficult to untangle and deploy.

AI-DLC introduces **Units**. Unlike purely conceptual boundaries, a Unit is a highly cohesive, self-contained element engineered from the ground up for independent deployment and measurable business value. 

Because AI excels at orchestrating webs of complex micro-interactions, it effortlessly manages smaller, modular, and decoupled Units. They aren't just theoretical boundaries; they are the fundamental, deployable building blocks that allow AI to assemble, swap, and scale systems with unprecedented agility.
